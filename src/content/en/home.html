What errors are most common in Russian versions of international company websites?

Russian versions of international company websites most often contain spelling, grammar, and punctuation errors, incorrect cases and word agreement, literal machine translation, incorrect terminology, and unnatural phrasing. Untranslated buttons, menus, form messages, leftover template text, and other interface elements are also common.

In addition to text errors, customers are affected by website functionality errors: a link leads to the wrong page, a menu item opens an empty page, a request form does not work correctly, or prices, phone numbers, and other data differ between the Russian version and the main version of the website. According to Stanford University, typos and broken links damage trust in a website more than many companies realize.

What is the Customer Trust in Website Score (CTWS), and what does it measure?

The Customer Trust in Website Score (CTWS) is EPG Global ONE’s proprietary tool: a score from 0 to 100 that shows how much the errors found on a website may reduce a customer’s trust in the company and make it harder for them to contact the company or make a purchase. Five criteria are considered: language and grammar, meaning and accuracy, clarity for the customer, presentation, and website functionality.

The score turns the website audit results into one clear metric. The company immediately sees the overall condition of the website, and during repeat audits it can track changes over time, evaluate the results of corrections, and see how website changes and updates affect customer trust.

Scoring starts at 100 points, and points are deducted for each error depending on its importance: 10 points for a critical error, 4 points for a serious error, and 2 points for a minor error.

Below 50 points means errors seriously undermine customer trust; 50 to 69 means noticeable errors make it harder for customers to trust the website; 70 to 89 means individual errors reduce the overall impression of the website; 90 to 100 means the website is perceived well and customer trust is high.
