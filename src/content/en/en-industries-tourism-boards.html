Website Audits for National Tourism Boards

Travelers start getting to know a country on its official tourism website: destinations, entry requirements, and news. Errors and outdated information on such a website undermine trust in the country as a travel destination. We have reviewed websites of national and regional tourism boards.

Results from our review of 5 tourism board websites in 2026

  • 4 of 5websites had language errors and signs of machine translation
  • 3 of 5websites had text left in English or the local language
  • 3 of 5websites had outdated information, such as news about borders reopening after COVID-19
  • 3 of 5websites had leftover template text in plain view
