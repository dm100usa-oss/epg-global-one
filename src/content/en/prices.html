Prices are listed for one language version of a website. You can start with a pilot audit: it shows the scale of the problems and the Customer Trust in Website Score (CTWS).

What We Audit

1. Russian Website Version Audit

We audit the Russian version of a website or individual Russian-language pages through the eyes of a Russian-speaking customer: language, grammar, signs of machine translation, untranslated elements, links, and customer reviews. A native speaker immediately notices errors that the company may miss.

2. Full Website Audit

We audit websites in any language: incorrect links, outdated pages, placeholders, system code, and differences between language versions. These errors gradually accumulate during website updates and often remain unnoticed by the company, but not by customers.
