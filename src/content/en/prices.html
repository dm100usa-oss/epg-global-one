What Every Audit Includes

One Audit Instead of Four Separate Checks

Companies usually hire four different people to review a website: a translator, a proofreader, a web specialist, and a marketer. We do it all in one project, for one price.

  • Language and grammar
  • Signs of machine translation, meaning, and tone
  • Untranslated buttons, menus, and text
  • Links, navigation, and language switching
  • Request forms and contact buttons
  • Prices, phone numbers, and figures checked against the main version
  • Outdated pages, placeholders, and leftover system text
  • For every error: where it is and how to fix it

Subscription: We Take Website Quality Control Off Your Hands

Why it pays off. Your website changes all the time: new prices, doctors, promotions, and pages. Errors creep in unnoticed and pile up, and customers leave without a word. With a subscription, a new error is caught almost immediately instead of sitting on your site for months.

Once a week, we review your website ourselves, find new and changed pages, check them, and send you what needs fixing. You don’t need to send us anything or count pages.
